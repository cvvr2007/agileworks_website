Compliance Framework

Our 5 Pillars of Enterprise Financial Governance

Designed for seamless vendor onboarding and audit readiness.

1. Institutional Multi-Currency Settlements

We minimize cross-border payment friction by leveraging secure, RBI-authorized payment channels that provide transparent, mid-market exchange rates.

2. Regional Ledger & Tax Architecture

Our multi-currency ledger framework is designed to properly classify international service exports in accordance with applicable zero-rated tax regulations.

3. Automated Invoicing & LUT Compliance

All engagements are billed through our integrated Zoho Books portal. International invoices automatically include mandatory statutory declarations ("Supply meant for export under Bond/LUT").

4. Commercial Governance & Contracting

We operate with transparent, standardized terms: 14-day proposal validity, Net 15 payment terms, and clear Intellectual Property (IP) transfer clauses tied to financial clearance.

5. Compliance Execution & e-FIRAs

End-to-end inbound cashflow reconciliation (T+1 settlement). We automatically generate and provide digital Foreign Inward Remittance Advice (e-FIRA) within 24 hours of settlement.
