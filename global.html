Compliance Framework

Our 5 Pillars of Enterprise Financial Compliance

Designed for seamless vendor onboarding and audit readiness.

1. Frictionless Multi-Currency Payments

We eliminate cross-border payment friction. Through secure, RBI-authorized payment partners (including Xflow), we accept payments in major global currencies with live mid-market exchange rates and zero hidden FX markups.

2. Automated, Compliant Invoicing

All engagements are billed through our integrated Zoho Books portal. International invoices are automatically generated with proper statutory declarations (e.g., "Supply meant for export under Bond/LUT"), saving your procurement team hours of manual compliance checks.

3. Strict Commercial Governance

We operate with transparent, standardized commercial terms: 14-day proposal validity, Net 15 payment terms, and clear Intellectual Property (IP) ownership transfer clauses that activate immediately upon financial clearance.

4. Instant Audit & Compliance Documentation

We understand enterprise audit requirements. Upon payment settlement (typically T+1), we automatically generate and provide digital Foreign Inward Remittance Advice (e-FIRA) and all necessary tax exemption certificates within 24 hours.

5. Secure Data & Contract Execution

All vendor onboarding, NDA execution, and contract signatures are handled via secure, legally binding electronic contract platforms, ensuring rapid vendor setup without compromising your security standards.
